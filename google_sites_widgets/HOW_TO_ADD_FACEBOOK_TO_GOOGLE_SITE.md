## 🌐 1. On Your Google Site (`https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home`)

You have three clean options depending on where you want the Facebook link to appear:

### Option A: Use Google Sites Built-In "Social links" (Native & Clean - 30 seconds)
1. Open your Google Site editor: **[https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home](https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home)**.
2. In the right panel, make sure the **Insert** tab is selected.
3. Scroll down through the widgets and click **Social links**.
4. In the popup:
   - In the first link box, paste:  
     `https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61585237073673`
   - Google Sites will automatically detect Facebook and display the official Facebook logo.
5. Choose your style preferences (e.g., Circle, Filled, Medium size).
6. Click **Insert**, then drag the social links strip to your footer or header.
7. Click **Publish**!
