# How to Embed the Flagship Project Video on Google Sites (Home Page)

This guide explains how to embed the **Greenhouse Water Circulation & Pollutant Transport to Receiving Waters** flagship project video on your Google Site Home page (`https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home`).

---

## ⚡ Option 1: High-Tech HTML Embed Widget (Recommended)

This provides a custom-designed research card with a cyan-neon glowing border, project metadata grid, topic chips, and the crisp **title cover image poster from the last 5 seconds** (`project_circulation_cover.webp`).

### ⏱️ Steps:

1. **Open your Google Site editor**:
   - Go to: **[https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home](https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home)** (or open your Google Sites editor and navigate to the **Home** page).

2. **Click Embed**:
   - In the right-hand panel, click the **Insert** tab.
   - Click **Embed** (the `< >` icon).
   - Select the **Embed code** tab.

3. **Copy & Paste the Widget Code**:
   - Open [`google_sites_widgets/widget_project_circulation.html`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Modeling%20work/AI_Water_Guard_Dashboard_All_Files/google_sites_widgets/widget_project_circulation.html).
   - Copy everything inside that file (`Ctrl+A`, then `Ctrl+C`).
   - Paste it into the Google Sites **Embed code** box.
   - Click **Next**, verify the preview with the cover image, and click **Insert**.

4. **Align & Resize**:
   - Position this widget **at the very top** above the other videos (above the Drone telemetry showcase and MBC news broadcast).
   - Drag the blue sizing circles on the left and right edges so the card fills the full width of your page.
   - Drag the bottom handle downward until the video, metadata cards, and buttons are fully visible without internal scrollbars (~750px to 820px).

5. **Publish**:
   - Click the **Publish** button at the top right of Google Sites!

---

## 📁 Option 2: Direct Google Drive Video Upload

1. **Upload to Google Drive**:
   - Upload `rwesl_website/assets/videos/greenhouse_water_circulation_project.mp4` (or `Project .mp4`) to your Google Drive.
   - Right-click the file > **Share** > set to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.

2. **Insert into Google Sites**:
   - Go to **Insert** > **Drive**.
   - Select your uploaded video and insert it at the top of other videos on the Home page.
