# How to Add Widget #1 (Hero & Stats Hub) to Your Google Site

## ⏱️ Takes less than 1 minute:

1. **Open your Google Site editor**:
   Go to: `https://sites.google.com/view/rwer/home` (or click **Edit** in Google Sites).

2. **Open the Embed Tool**:
   - In the right-hand panel, click **Insert**.
   - Click the **Embed** button (looks like `< >`).
   - Select the **Embed code** tab (not "By URL").

3. **Paste the Code**:
   - Open [widget_1_hero_stats.html](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Modeling%20work/AI_Water_Guard_Dashboard_All_Files/google_sites_widgets/widget_1_hero_stats.html).
   - Copy **everything** inside that file (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C).
   - Paste it into the Google Sites **Embed code** box.
   - Click **Next**, then click **Insert**.

4. **Adjust the Size**:
   - Drag the blue handles on the corners so the widget fills the entire page width.
   - Set the height so the entire card set and status bar are visible without internal scrollbars.

5. **Publish**:
   - Click **Publish** at the top right of Google Sites!
