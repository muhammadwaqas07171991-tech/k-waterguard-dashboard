Key Hydrological Principles Demonstrated in the Animation

🔹 Spatial Rainfall Variation: Illustrates non-uniform precipitation intensity distribution across mountain basin topography.
🔹 Area-Weighted Rainfall Averaging: Delineates concentric isohyetal rings to calculate representative areal precipitation depth.
🔹 Moving Time-Window Analysis: Scans continuous storm hyetographs to identify peak precipitation epochs for various critical durations.
🔹 Maximum Rainfall Depth Extraction: Envelopes maximum depth bounds for 2h, 4h, and 6h severe storm events.
🔹 Development and Interpretation of DAD Curves: Transforms complex numerical storm statistics into actionable design curves for hydraulic infrastructure sizing, dam safety, and AI flood risk prevention.
